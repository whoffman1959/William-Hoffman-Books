The Ascendancy Wars

The Emma Shard

Book One

Planets fall. Heroes rise. The fate of humanity balances on a knife-edge.

The Emma Shard is a fast-paced military science-fiction adventure where every decision could change the future of mankind. Beneath the dust of a dead civilization, something still listens—and learns.

When an ancient enemy goes silent, new threats emerge from the shadows. Worlds burn, loyalties fracture, and soldiers and outcasts must choose whether to stand their ground or be swept into history.
