Daily Reflection for the Soul

A Spirit's Love

Reflections from the Book of Acts

Step into the world of the early church and follow the movement of the gospel from Jerusalem outward.

A Spirit's Love is a 90-day journey through the Book of Acts, exploring how the Holy Spirit empowered ordinary believers with courage, wisdom, and boldness to carry the message of Christ into a changing world.

Each reflection combines Scripture, historical context, personal application, thoughtful questions, and prayer, helping readers connect the work of the early church with the call to faithful living today.
