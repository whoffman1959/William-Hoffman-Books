A Son's Love

Reflections from the Gospel of John

What if the Light of the world was not just a historical event, but a daily presence in your heart?

A Son's Love invites you into a 90-day journey through the Gospel of John, exploring the life and words of Jesus Christ—the Word made flesh and the Light that darkness cannot overcome.

Each daily reflection combines Scripture, personal application, thoughtful questions, and prayer, helping you move beyond simply reading the Gospel to living in the truth it reveals.
